Using AI Without Compromising Student Privacy

AI can be a powerful analysis partner, but student data has to stay protected. The line is simple, and once you know where it sits, a whole set of safe, high-value uses opens up.

🔒

The golden rule: never put personally identifiable student information into an AI tool. No names, no student IDs, no email addresses, nothing traceable to an individual. De-identify first, then analyze.

Sort it out: what's safe, what's off-limits
✓ Generally Safe
  • 📸Photos or screenshots of student work with names and identifiers cropped or covered
  • 📊Grade and assignment patterns after names and IDs are stripped and replaced with labels like Student A, B, C
  • 📝Your own materials: rubrics, prompts, lesson plans, assignment instructions, syllabus language
  • 🧩De-identified, aggregated trends: "60% missed question 4," not "Jordan missed question 4"
✕ Off-Limits
  • 🪪Anything with a student name, ID number, or email still attached
  • 📂Uploading a raw gradebook export straight from your system without cleaning it first
  • 🖼️Screenshots where a name, photo, or login is visible in a corner or header
  • ⚠️Small-class data so unique it could re-identify someone even without a name attached
