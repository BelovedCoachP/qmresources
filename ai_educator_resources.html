🎙️ AI Voice Interaction Use Cases

1
Chat with Historical Figures / Experts
Bring your subject matter to life through dialogue

Students have a voice conversation with AI playing a historical figure, author, scientist, or expert in your field. They must think critically to ask good questions and engage meaningfully with the content.

Example Prompt

"You are Frederick Douglass. I'm a journalism student interviewing you about the role of the press in social change. Stay in character and respond as Douglass would, drawing on your writings and speeches. Ask me questions too."

History Literature Philosophy Political Science
2
Language Practice Partner
24/7 conversation practice without judgment

AI becomes a patient, always-available conversation partner for language learners. It can adjust difficulty, correct mistakes gently, and simulate real-world scenarios like ordering food or job interviews.

Example Prompt

"You are a friendly Spanish tutor. Have a conversation with me about ordering food at a restaurant. Speak only in Spanish, but if I make a grammar mistake, gently correct me and explain why in English, then continue in Spanish."

Foreign Languages ESL/ELL Communication
3
Devil's Advocate / Stress Test Ideas
Strengthen thinking through rigorous challenge

AI argues against the student's position, research proposal, or project plan. This forces students to anticipate objections, identify weaknesses, and strengthen their arguments before presenting to others.

Example Prompt

"I'm going to explain my research proposal. Your job is to be a skeptical reviewer—ask tough questions, point out weaknesses, and challenge my assumptions. Don't be mean, but be rigorous. After we're done, summarize the main concerns I should address."

Research Methods Business Debate Thesis Prep
4
Reverse Interview / Socratic Dialogue
AI asks questions to draw out student thinking

Instead of students asking AI questions, the AI asks the student questions to help them discover what they know and don't know. Builds metacognition and articulation skills.

Example Prompt

"I want to explore my understanding of photosynthesis. Ask me probing questions one at a time—don't give me answers, help me discover what I know and don't know through your questions. Start simple and get more complex based on my responses."

Any Subject Test Prep Self-Assessment
5
Role-Play Simulations
Practice real-world professional scenarios

Students practice professional conversations: patient intake, client meetings, difficult workplace conversations, interviews. AI can play multiple roles and provide feedback afterward.

Example Prompt

"You are a patient coming into a clinic with chest pain. You're anxious and not giving clear answers. I'm a nursing student practicing patient intake. After our conversation, break character and give me feedback on my communication skills."

Nursing Social Work Business Counseling
6
Creative Storytelling / World Building
Collaborative narrative creation

Build stories together through voice conversation. Great for creative writing classes, exploring "what if" scenarios in history, or making abstract concepts tangible through narrative.

Example Prompt

"Let's create a story together set during the Industrial Revolution. You'll be the narrator and various characters. I'll be a factory worker. Describe scenes vividly and give me choices about what my character does next."

Creative Writing History Theater Game Design

💡 Pro Tips for Custom GPTs

Search Smart

In the GPT Store, search terms like "instructional design," "higher education," "professor," or "curriculum" to find relevant assistants.

Build Your Own

Create custom GPTs with your syllabus, rubrics, and course materials uploaded. They'll reference your specific context in every response.

Combine with Voice

Many custom GPTs work great with ChatGPT's voice mode—talk through your course design instead of typing.
